# Hi, I'm Ilhan Erdogan

Founder of **[Erdora](https://erdora.fr)**, a small web studio based in Toulon, France. I design, code and launch websites for craftspeople, shops and small businesses: clear on a phone, fast, and built to be found on Google.

I'm also a third-year computer science and digital engineering student at ISEN Méditerranée (Toulon), with a semester at UniKL MIIT in Malaysia.

## What I build

- **[erdora.fr](https://erdora.fr)**: my own studio's website. A pink brain releases an "idea cable" that follows you as you scroll and plugs into each section, all the way to the quote button. Pre-rendered pages, no cookies, no third-party requests.
- **[aghrenovation.fr](https://aghrenovation.fr)**: website for a masonry and renovation company in the Var. Its trades, 35 project photos sorted by type of work, Google reviews, the towns it serves, a quote form and a light/dark mode.
- **Motion videos** for my clients, made with Remotion, ElevenLabs voices and sound design.
- **Games on Roblox**, scripted in Lua.
- **[Pokedex](https://github.com/Eoppl/Pokedex)**: a mobile development project.
